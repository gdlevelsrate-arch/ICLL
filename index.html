WHAT COUNTS?

ICLL V9.1's automatic submission checks.

💀 PHYSICALLY IMPOSSIBLE

A challenge that cannot be completed under its defined game conditions.

⚡ THEORETICALLY POSSIBLE

A challenge with a theoretical solution that may exceed realistic human ability.

Minimum duration: 5 seconds. Include a name, creator, numeric level ID and a meaningful explanation. A video link is optional.

SUBMIT A CHALLENGE

Check your level against the submission rules.

V9.1 demo: submissions are saved only in your own browser. They are not sent to the site owner or shared with other visitors. Automatic checks cannot prove that a level is impossible.

🤖 AUTO REVIEW BOT

🟢 Passed checks: the form meets the automatic requirements, but a human still needs to verify the gameplay.

🟡 Needs details: a submission is missing required information. Passing checks does not verify the gameplay.

🔴 Rejected: one or more automatic requirements failed.

This version does not use a server, database or Discord bot. To collect submissions from everyone, you'll need a connected submission service or backend.
