WHAT COUNTS?

Draft categories — final acceptance and ranking rules will be decided by the list team.

💀 PHYSICALLY IMPOSSIBLE

Challenges with no valid completion under the defined game version, physics, settings and allowed inputs. Claims should include evidence or an explanation.

⚡ THEORETICALLY POSSIBLE

Challenges that have a valid solution in theory but are considered beyond realistic human ability. Tool-assisted demonstrations can help document them.

⏱️ Length rule: Minimum 5 seconds; 5–15 seconds recommended. Longer challenges are allowed.

🎥 Video evidence rule: Must have a video if the challenge is almost impossible, even for robots.

Important: An unsolved challenge is not automatically physically impossible. Different game versions or mechanics can change whether a level is possible.
